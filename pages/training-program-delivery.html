Training Program Delivery

Number of training programs conducted or rolled out by the facilitator.

How this KRA is scored

Formula
Programs Delivered / Programs Required × 100%
Target
At least 2 programs per month.

Data source

Each session is logged as one row — Session ID, date, program/module, training type, brand, store, facilitator(s), target/actual pax, duration, status, and post-test average — in the Training Program & Delivery Monitoring 2026 sheet, in the KPI Data sheet (Pilot) Drive folder.
