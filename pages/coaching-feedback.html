    How this report was built

    Source. Figaro Culinary Group – Store Visit Evaluation Survey Form (RESPONSE), "Form responses 1" tab, responses.

    Name matching. The Facilitator / Trainer field is free text, so spellings were grouped: "Sir Leo", "Leo Rey Fernandez", "loe", "lea" → Leo Fernandez; "Ver", "Jeliver", "Gerero" → Ver Guerrero; "Nika", "Nina" → Nica Tardio; "Anne", "an" → Ann Barredo; "Deniel", "Deleon" → Daniel De Leon; "Denis", "Denise" → Dennis; "Joshua" → Josh Earnshaw; "Rice" → Ricelle Lim. Sky, Dennis and Jov Palattao are listed as written in the responses.

    Team Effort. Entries with no individual name ("Training Department", "Trainer", "Facilitator", "Training Room", "Trainee", "?", "Good", a store or other department name, or unrelated text) are credited to Team Effort.

    Shared sessions. When a response names several facilitators, each one receives full credit for that response. Because of this, facilitator totals add up to more than the number of responses.

    KPIs. Average rating = mean of all 15 items. Delivery = objectives, expertise, organization, questions answered, participation. Content = relevance, practical examples, knowledge gained, operational improvement. Impact = the six learning-impact statements. Satisfaction = share rating 4 or 5 on "Overall, I am satisfied with the activity".

    Data fixes. Three activity dates were invalid (08/06/2026, 03/09/0026, 11/07/2000) and were replaced with the submission date. Six responses with a blank or unclear activity were assigned the activity recorded at the same store or by the same facilitator that day.

    Scale misreads.
